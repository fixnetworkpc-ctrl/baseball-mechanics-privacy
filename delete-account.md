## How to delete your account

There are two ways. Either one deletes the same data.

### 1. In the app (immediate)

1. Open Baseball Mechanics and tap the **settings** icon.
2. Scroll to the **bottom** of the settings panel to the section headed **DELETE ACCOUNT**.
3. Tap **Delete Account** and confirm.

This cannot be undone.

### 2. By email

If you can no longer open the app — for example you have already uninstalled it — email
**mechanicssupport@gmail.com** from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it.

We respond within **30 days**.

---

## ⚠️ Uninstalling the app does not delete your account

Removing the app deletes the copy of your data stored **on that device**. It does **not** delete
your cloud account or the data stored with it.

On **iPhone and iPad** this is especially important: your sign-in token is kept in the system
keychain, which Apple does not clear when an app is removed. Reinstalling therefore returns you
to your own account and history rather than a blank one. That is deliberate — but it means
uninstalling is not deletion. Use one of the two routes above.

## What is deleted

When you delete your account, we delete:

- Your account and profile, including your name and date of birth
- Your email address and cloud account
- Your full analysis and session history
- Your recruiting profile, including grad year, positions, school, travel team, height and
  weight, bats/throws, GPA and test scores if you entered them, measurables, and mechanics scores
- Your verification records
- Your marketing preference and the campaign code you arrived through
- Team data you own

**Video frames are never part of this, because they are never kept.** Frames you submit for
analysis are discarded as soon as the analysis is generated. The video file itself never leaves
your device at any point.

**Player photos are never part of this either** — they stay on your device and are never
uploaded.

## What may be kept, and for how long

| Data | Retention after deletion |
|---|---|
| **Diagnostic reports** | Up to **12 months** from when they were created, then deleted. These are crash and error reports used to keep the app working; they are not tied to your profile. |
| **Team data owned by someone else** | If you were a member of a team another person created, that team continues to exist. Your own membership and scores are removed with your account. |
| **Records we are required to keep** | Anything we must retain to meet a legal or accounting obligation, for as long as that obligation lasts. |

Everything else is deleted.

> **A note on verification records.** While your account exists, a verification record is kept as
> an audit trail even after the measurable it describes is edited or removed — its whole purpose
> is to record what a coach confirmed and when. **Those records are deleted with your account.**

## Deleting a child's data

A parent or legal guardian may review or delete their child's data at any time by emailing
**mechanicssupport@gmail.com** from the guardian address on the account. We respond within
30 days.

A guardian may also **withdraw consent** at any time, using the link in the confirmation email we
sent when consent was given, or from the pending-consent screen inside the app. Withdrawing
consent locks the child's account and then deletes the child's data.

If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental
consent, we delete it promptly.

## Other options

If you do not want to delete your whole account:

- **Turn off a recruiting profile** — change its visibility or delete just the profile, in the
  app, at any time. Lowering visibility stops it being served at the higher level immediately.
- **Withdraw marketing consent** — email us; it takes effect immediately, and your account stays.
- **Request a copy of your data** — email us and we will send you one.
