## Introduction

Baseball Mechanics ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application that uses artificial intelligence to analyze baseball pitching, hitting, and catching mechanics. Many of the players analyzed in this App are children, and we treat that as the central fact of this policy rather than a footnote.

This policy covers the Baseball Mechanics mobile app for iOS and Android **and the companion web portal** we operate for the same accounts. Both are backed by the same database and the same servers — see Section 12.

It explains what we collect, where it is stored, who processes it, and what you can do about it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide at Setup

- **Player name** (required) — personalizes analysis reports.
- **Your date of birth** (required) — the date of birth of the person setting up and owning the account. We use it to verify your own age and to determine when the protections in Section 5 apply. It cannot be skipped, because the protections in Section 5 are impossible to apply to an account whose owner's age we never asked for.
- **The player's date of birth** (optional) — collected only when the account holder is setting the App up for someone else, such as a parent or coach entering a player. It is used solely to place the player in the right age division and to age-calibrate their analysis. It is stored **on your device with that player's profile**, not on your account record.
- **Email address** (optional for account holders 13 and over; **required** for under-13 accounts, where it must be the parent's or guardian's — see Section 5).
- **Marketing preference** — whether you ticked the optional box to receive product-update emails. It is off unless you turn it on.

We no longer ask you to declare whether you are a player, a coach, or a parent. Earlier versions of the App collected that as a "role"; it was removed, and the App does not record it for new accounts.

### 1.2 Account

The App creates an account for you. If you provide an email address you receive a cloud account (sign-in by one-time code; we never see or store a password). If you do not provide one, the App signs you in **anonymously** — a random identifier with no personal information attached to it.

**On iOS, your account identifier deliberately survives deleting the App.** We store your sign-in token in the iOS Keychain, which Apple does not clear when an app is removed, so reinstalling returns you to the same account and the same history rather than a blank one. See Section 6 for what this means for deletion.

The web portal signs in with an email address and a password you set, because a browser cannot receive a one-time code the way the App can. It is the same account either way.

We do not collect payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or precise location.

### 1.3 Video Frames You Submit for Analysis

You select a video from your device and the App extracts still frames at specific mechanical positions. **Those still images — not the original video — are sent to our server for AI analysis, and they are not retained after the analysis completes.** We never receive or store the original video file, and there is no library, archive, or training set of user video anywhere in our systems.

The frames are analyzed for body position and movement. They are not used for facial recognition and are not matched against any identity database.

**Please do not capture frames containing people other than the player being analyzed.**

### 1.4 Analysis and Session History

Your analysis results (mechanics scores, identified strengths and weaknesses, recommended drills) are stored on your device. If you have a cloud account, they are **also stored on our servers** so they are available across your devices and over time.

### 1.5 Recruiting Profile (optional)

If you choose to create one, we store and sync the fields you fill in. That is more than a name and a position, so here is the full list:

- **Athletic and identifying:** graduation year, height, weight, bats/throws, primary position, secondary position
- **Affiliation:** high school, travel ball team, state
- **Academic:** GPA and SAT/ACT scores, if you enter them
- **Measurables:** 60-yard dash, exit velocity, pitch velocity, pop time, if you enter them
- **Your mechanics scores**, carried over from your analysis history
- **Your chosen visibility setting** (Section 4)

Every one of these is optional and self-entered — leave any of them blank and we store nothing for it. Recruiting profiles are stored in our cloud database so they can be shown to the people you choose in Section 4.

**This feature is switched off entirely for players under 13 and cannot be turned on.**

**Player photos are not part of this.** If you add a photo to a player's profile, it stays on your device. We do not upload it, and there is no photo storage in our systems.

### 1.6 Coach Verification of Measurables (optional)

A self-entered measurable is a claim. If an athlete wants one confirmed, they can ask a coach to verify it, and that creates records:

- The athlete supplies **the coach's email address** and we email that coach a one-time link. That address is a third party's personal information, provided to us by the athlete — please only enter the address of a coach who expects to hear from you.
- We store **the measurable, the exact value at the time it was submitted, the outcome, any note the coach adds, and the date**. The value is snapshotted deliberately: if the athlete later edits the number, the record still shows what was actually verified, rather than lending a coach's name to a different figure.
- If the coach has an account, we store **their name and their school or team** so a verification can be attributed and can outlive their move to another club.

**Verification is not offered to athletes under 14**, and it is withheld when we cannot establish an age. Verification records are an audit trail and are kept even if the underlying profile is later changed — see Section 6.

### 1.7 Team Data (optional)

If a coach creates a team, we store the roster and the members' mechanics scores so the coach can see team-level progress.

### 1.8 Subscription Status

If you purchase a subscription, we store **whether you have an active subscription and which one**. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google — **we never see or store your payment details.**

### 1.9 Diagnostic Information

If the App hits a technical error we receive a report containing the error, the screen it happened on, your OS and app version, and your name and email if you provided one. Used only to fix bugs; never sold, and never used for marketing.

### 1.10 How You Found Us

If you arrive through a campaign link, a printed QR code, or a code you type in at setup, we record **which code it was and which of those routes it came through**. We use it to understand which outreach works. It grants nothing, unlocks nothing, and is not linked to anything you do in the App beyond the fact that you arrived.

If you reached us through our website first, that code may be held in a cookie on our own site so it survives the trip to the app store. It is a first-party cookie for that single purpose. We run no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers.

### 1.11 What We Do NOT Collect

- Precise or approximate GPS location
- Contacts, call logs, or messages
- Browsing activity outside our own app and website
- Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data
- Biometric identifiers, facial recognition data, or voiceprints
- Payment card or bank account information

## 4. Recruiting Profile Visibility — You Control This

If you create a recruiting profile, **you choose** who can see it:

- **Private** — nobody but you.
- **Coaches only** — the coaches of teams you belong to. This is the maximum for any player under 13.
- **Recruiters** — visible to recruiter accounts in the App's search and discovery tools. Those tools require a signed-in account; they are not open to the public web.
- **Public** — additionally reachable through a shareable link you can send to a college coach who does not use the App. The link contains a long random identifier; it cannot be guessed, but **anyone you send it to can open it**, so share it deliberately.

You can change the visibility or delete the profile at any time. Lowering the visibility immediately stops it being served at the higher level.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

**Baseball Mechanics is used to analyze children, and we designed this section on the assumption that the player is a minor unless we know otherwise.**

### 5.1 Date of birth is required

We ask for **the account holder's date of birth** at setup and it cannot be skipped. That is the date the protections below are keyed to, because the account holder is the person who agrees to this policy, owns the account, and receives our email. Whether that person is under 13 is determined from this date of birth, and **that determination is calculated and enforced on our servers, not left to the app alone.**

If the account holder is setting the App up for someone else, they may also give **the player's date of birth**. That one is optional, it stays on the device with that player's profile, and it is used only to place the player in the right age division and age-calibrate their scoring. If it is not given, the App asks about the player's age at analysis time instead.

If we cannot establish a player's age, we apply the **stricter** treatment, not the looser one — an unknown age is treated as a child, and features gated on age stay switched off.

### 5.2 How a parent or guardian gives verifiable consent.

If the account holder's date of birth shows they are under 13, the account can only be set up by a parent or legal guardian. Setup does not activate the account. Instead:

1. The guardian provides their own email address, which becomes the owner of the account and must be verified by a one-time code we email at sign-in.
2. We then email that address a unique, single-use confirmation link. The account stays locked and cannot be used until the guardian clicks it. Clicking the link is the guardian's verifiable consent, and we record the date and time it occurred.
3. Shortly after, we send a second confirmation email to the same address, which also contains a link to withdraw consent at any time.

This "email-plus" process is a verifiable parental consent method permitted under COPPA because a child's information in this App is never disclosed publicly — every under-13 player is restricted to coach-only visibility (see Section 5.1). Until a guardian completes step 2, no analysis, profile, or other data for that player is used beyond what is strictly necessary to hold the pending account.

### 5.3 Under-13 players are never published

For any player under 13, **recruiter and public visibility are switched off and cannot be turned on.** Their recruiting profile can be shared with the coaches of their own team and no further. It is never listed in recruiter search, never returned by discovery, and never reachable by a public link. This is enforced in the App and independently enforced again on our server, so it cannot be bypassed by a modified client.

Coach verification of measurables (Section 1.6) is not offered below age 14 at all, and is withheld entirely when the age is unknown.

### 5.4 Marketing

We do not send marketing email to children. The only address on an under-13 account is the guardian's, and it receives product-update email only if the guardian themselves opted in.

### 5.5 Parental rights

A parent or guardian may review or delete their child's data at any time by emailing **mechanicssupport@gmail.com** from the guardian address on the account. We respond within 30 days. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we delete it promptly.
A guardian may also withdraw consent instantly using the link in the confirmation email, or from within the app's pending-consent screen; doing so locks and then deletes the child's account and data.

## 7. Data Security

- All traffic between the App and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS.
- Our database enforces row-level security: your rows are readable only by your account.
- API keys and credentials live in server-side environment variables and are never shipped in the App.
- Verification links are single-use and time-limited, and we store only a hash of the link's token — never the token itself.
- Cloud backups are encrypted.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

**Everyone:**

- **Access** — email us for a copy of your data.
- **Deletion** — [request account deletion](https://fixnetworkpc-ctrl.github.io/baseball-mechanics-privacy/delete-account), in the app or by email, and we will delete your account and its cloud data. Uninstalling removes the local copy but **not** the cloud copy.
- **Correction** — edit your profile in the App.
- **Withdraw marketing consent** — email us; it takes effect immediately.
- **Control recruiting visibility** — change it or delete the profile in the App at any time (Section 4).

**California residents (CCPA):** you may know what we collect, request deletion, and opt out of sale — **we do not sell personal information** — without being discriminated against for exercising these rights.

**EEA / UK residents (GDPR):** you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability. Our legal bases are **consent** (email, marketing, recruiting publication, verification), **contract** (delivering the analysis you asked for), and **legitimate interest** (diagnostics, security, and knowing which outreach brought you here). Where the user is a child, consent is given by the holder of parental responsibility.

To exercise any of these, contact **mechanicssupport@gmail.com**.
